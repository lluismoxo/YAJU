Why this is a challenge

The four areas are really one problem: agents are easy to create and hard to operate. That distance is the reason Yaju exists.

Easy to create

Models, frameworks and tools have made the first working agent cheap to build. That is why so many of them exist, and why the count keeps growing inside every organisation.

Hard to operate

The difficulty arrives afterwards: no named owner, no cost attributed, no policy applied at the moment of action, and no way to prove what an agent did or what it spent.

Why Yaju exists

The Yaju Agent Orchestration System covers the part that begins after the demo, so that agents can be created, used, managed and improved like production software.
