YAJU AS AI Approach

Artificial intelligence is at the center of YAJU AS. This page explains, in simple terms, how AI is used on the Platform, who is responsible for what, and the principles we follow.

What YAJU AS is

YAJU AS is a platform to create, import and manage AI agents. We do not build AI models: the models belong to the providers you choose, such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Higgsfield, Artlist or ElevenLabs.

Our role is to give you one place to run, control and understand your agents: their status, their activity, their costs and their settings.

Our principles

  • Human control: people, not agents, should have the final word on important decisions.
  • Transparency: we explain where and how AI is used in the Platform.
  • Respect for your data: we do not train AI models with your data and we do not sell it.
  • Responsible use: we do not allow harmful uses of AI on the Platform.

You stay in control

  • You choose the models, connect your own keys and decide which tools each agent can use.
  • You decide the permissions and autonomy of each agent, and you are responsible for the actions it takes.
  • You can stop or delete your agents at any time.
  • AI can make mistakes. Review agent results before using them, especially for important decisions.

Limits of AI

AI models can produce results that are wrong, incomplete, biased or out of date, even when they sound confident. Agents can also take unexpected actions if their instructions are unclear or their permissions are too wide. This is why we recommend clear instructions, limited permissions and human review of important results.

Where we use AI ourselves

Some Platform features, such as the Agent Analyzer and Reviewer, use AI models to analyze your agents and suggest improvements. Their results are suggestions and can be wrong. You decide whether to apply them.

Your data

We do not train AI models with your data and we do not sell it. See our Data Commitments.

EU AI Act

We do not allow the practices prohibited by the EU AI Act (see our Usage Policy). If you use agents for high-risk purposes under the AI Act, you are responsible for the obligations that apply to that use.
