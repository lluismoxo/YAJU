Frequently asked questions

  • Yaju started in 2024. A group of computer engineers needed to use, deploy and manage AI agents in their daily work, and the tools on the market could not do it: there was no way to control what an agent was allowed to do, no way to know what it cost, and no way to prove what it had done. They built what was missing, and that became the Yaju Agent Orchestration System. Yaju AS is operated by CAP CONSULTOR LLC, from Barcelona.
