Open Science Initiative

Accelerating development through open collaboration

Yaju Labs advances AI agents through open development, bringing together the people who build, run and improve agents, and doing that work in the open.

Benefits:

  • Collaborate with the Yaju team

  • An exclusive network

  • Contribute to the Yaju ecosystem

Join our community and shape AI Agents with open development

  • You get to work side by side with the developers who build Yaju. Not at a distance and not through a form: in the same threads, on the same problems, with the people who maintain the Agent Orchestration System.

  • The community is a place to grow your own career. You take on real work, you get reviewed by people who do this every day, and what you build becomes something you can show. Many members arrive curious about agents and leave able to run them properly.

  • Members take part in Yaju projects directly. You can pick up an open piece of work, propose your own, and ship it alongside the team rather than around it.
