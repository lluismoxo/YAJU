Yaju Labs Open Science Community Application

Yaju Labs is a research lab that seeks to solve complex machine learning problems. In addition to our dedicated research team, we support cross-institutional collaborations and more entry points into ML research through this open science community.

We invite you to join us – we are researchers, engineers, linguists, social scientists, and lifelong learners that connect and collaborate with each other. We come together from over 100 countries around the world and support large and small-scale research collaborations. Shaped by our members' passions, we are driven by our ongoing commitment to support you as you pursue and achieve your research dreams. Join us. 

To facilitate our community’s supportive and collegial environment, we selectively add new members. Thank you for your patience while we individually review each application. We are grateful for your support of our open collaboration community as we work to change how research is done.
