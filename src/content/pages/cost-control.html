Three reasons the bill keeps growing

Alerts warn you. Circuit breakers stop the spend.

  • The invoice arrives grouped by provider, so nobody can say which agent caused the increase or which team owns it.

    • No visibility: the total is known, the cause is not
    • No attribution: spend grouped by provider, never by agent
    • No brakes: alerts fire once the money is already gone

  • Attribution by agent, real-time dashboards and hard budget limits that stop an overrun instead of reporting it.

    • Every run attributed to an agent, a user and a model
    • Warn at 80%, block at 100% until the cycle resets
    • Forecast the rest of the cycle from current usage

  • Evals are a cost lever, not just a quality one. They decide whether a cheaper configuration is actually acceptable.

    • Cost per execution before, after, and the delta in dollars
    • Quality delta measured against your baseline evals
    • Optimization results are measurable, not anecdotal
