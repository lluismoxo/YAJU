Frequently asked questions

  • An agent is one agent registered in Yaju that runs at least once in the month. Agents that do not run in a month are not billed.

  • Pricing is graduated. The first 20 agents cost $39 each per month, agents 21 to 300 cost $25 and agents 301 to 3,000 cost $15. Above 3,000 agents, or from 500 agents if you prefer an annual agreement, Enterprise pricing applies.

  • Governance, observability, budgets and traces, the credential vault and the MCP gateway, plus 30 sandbox hours and 8 browser hours a month.

  • Extra time is billed as you use it: $0.25 per sandbox hour and $0.20 per browser hour. Most agents that only call APIs never reach the included amount.

  • No. Your agents use your own model keys (BYOK), and your model provider bills you directly. Yaju attributes that spend to the agent, user and model that caused it.

  • Each Software Factory agent costs $149 per month and includes 160 sandbox hours and 20 browser hours. New accounts start with 100 million free tokens: 50 million on sign-up and 50 million once the Factory is configured.

  • The Agent Orchestration System and the Software Factory together, with volume pricing, an annual agreement, a dedicated success team and an SLA. Enterprise starts at 500 agents.

  • At the end of each calendar month, for the agents that ran and any extra sandbox or browser time.
