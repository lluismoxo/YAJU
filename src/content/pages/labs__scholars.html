About the program

The Scholars Program is a remote, part-time paid position that matches each scholar with a mentor. You work alongside the Yaju team on agent creation, use, management and improvement.

Mentorship

Work one to one with a mentor from the Yaju team for the whole length of the programme.

Tools

Get access to the Yaju ecosystem tools, the same ones our own engineers use to run agents.

Ownership

Everything you build during the programme is yours, and it stays under your own ownership.
