Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, which cookies we use on yajuas.com and capconsultor.eu, why we use them, and how you can control them.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser when you visit it. They allow the website to remember information about your visit, such as whether you are signed in or which cookie choices you made. Similar technologies, such as local storage or pixels, work in a similar way. In this policy we call all of them "cookies".

Cookies can be:

  • Necessary: needed for the website to work. They cannot be switched off.
  • Analytics: help us understand how visitors use the website. They are only used with your consent.

Cookies we use

yajuas.com and capconsultor.eu, operated by CAP Consultor LLC, use cookies and similar technologies. Necessary cookies are always on because the websites need them. Analytics cookies are only set if you accept them in our consent banner. You can change your choice at any time from the "Manage Cookies" link at the bottom of each page.

What Google Analytics collects

With your consent, Google Analytics collects data such as pages visited, time on page, clicks and other interactions, device and browser type, approximate location and the page you came from. Google Signals and advertising personalisation are disabled on yajuas.com. We use it to understand how our websites are used and to improve them. We do not sell it. Google may process it in the USA; see the Privacy Policy.

How to control cookies

You can accept, reject or change analytics cookies in our consent banner at any time. Rejecting analytics cookies does not affect your ability to use the websites or the Platform.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Each browser explains how to do it in its help section. If you block necessary cookies, the Platform may not work.

We may update this policy if we change the cookies we use. The date at the top shows the latest version. If we add new types of cookies that need your consent, we will ask for it again.
