About

Yaju AS builds the Agent Orchestration System: the layer companies use to deploy, run and govern their AI agents. Teams bring agents built with any framework, and Yaju gives each one an identity, an owner, credentials resolved at runtime, policy enforced at the moment of action, and a trace of everything it did.

The platform runs in our cloud, inside your own, or fully air-gapped, and it reports what every agent costs in currency rather than tokens. Yaju is operated by CAP CONSULTOR LLC, works from Barcelona and serves teams across Europe and beyond. Our Oran model generations power the work.
