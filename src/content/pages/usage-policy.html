Usage Policy

YAJU AS gives people and companies powerful tools: AI agents that can act, connect to other services and work with real data. To keep the Platform safe and useful for everyone, we need clear rules about how it can and cannot be used.

This policy explains what you may not do with YAJU AS. It applies to every user and to every agent run on the Platform, whether it was created in the Platform or imported. It is part of our Terms of Service. You must also follow the policies of the AI providers and tools you connect.

The examples below are not a complete list. If you are not sure whether a use is allowed, write to us before doing it.

1. Illegal or harmful use

  • Breaking any law, or helping others to break it.
  • Fraud, scams, phishing or impersonating people or organizations.
  • Harassment, threats, hate speech or incitement to violence.
  • Any content that sexualizes minors.
  • Helping to develop weapons, including chemical, biological, radiological or nuclear weapons.
  • Infringing intellectual property or privacy rights.
  • Collecting or processing personal data without a valid legal basis.

2. Security and platform abuse

  • Accessing systems, data or accounts without authorization, or testing their security without permission (see our Responsible Disclosure Policy).
  • Uploading malware, or code designed to escape the sandbox or reach other customers.
  • Mining cryptocurrency, mass scraping, spam or outbound attacks from the Platform.
  • Getting around plan limits, or loading the Platform abusively.
  • Reverse engineering, reselling or sublicensing the Platform without our permission.
  • Using YAJU AS resources (API keys, CLI, endpoints, brand) in a way that harms CAP's image.
  • Sharing accounts, or giving access to the Platform to people who have not accepted our Terms.

3. AI-specific rules

  • No practices prohibited by the EU AI Act, such as manipulative techniques that cause harm, exploiting people's vulnerabilities, social scoring, or emotion recognition at work or in education.
  • No fully automated decisions without human review in sensitive areas: health, legal, finance, insurance, employment, education, housing or safety.
  • Do not present AI-generated content as human-made where the law requires disclosure.
  • No deepfakes of real people without their consent, and no non-consensual intimate images.
  • No disinformation designed to deceive people, especially around elections.
  • Do not give an agent more permissions or autonomy than its task needs, and review what it does.
  • Do not use agents to get around the safety measures or usage policies of AI providers.

4. Media generation

When you use media generation platforms through YAJU AS, such as image, video or audio tools, you must also:

  • Respect the rights of other people over their image, voice and work.
  • Not create content that imitates a real person's voice or appearance without their consent.
  • Follow the content policies of the media generation platform you use.

5. Sanctions

The Platform may not be used by people or entities subject to international sanctions or located in sanctioned countries.

6. Your responsibility

You are responsible for your use of the Platform and for the actions of your agents, including agents used by members of your workspace. If you learn that your agents or your members are breaching this policy, you must stop it and tell us.

7. Enforcement

If we find a breach of this policy, we may remove content, stop agents, suspend or close the account, and report it to the authorities when appropriate. The measure will depend on how serious the breach is. In serious cases, such as illegal activity or attacks against the Platform or other customers, we may act immediately.

8. Report misuse

If you see a use of YAJU AS that breaches this policy, write to contact@capconsultor.eu. We reply within 5 business days.

9. Changes

We may update this policy to respond to new risks, new features or changes in the law. The date at the top shows the latest version. Important changes will be communicated as described in our Terms of Service.
