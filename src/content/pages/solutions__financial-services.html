Which agents run, what they did, and what they cost

Regulators and investment committees do not ask whether you use AI. They ask which agents touch client money and data, who approved them and whether you can prove it.

  • Research desks, operations and advisers build agents on their own, and some of them read portfolios and personal data. Yaju catalogues every agent, whatever framework built it, with an owner and a purpose.

    • Every agent registered with an owner and a purpose
    • Agents that reach client or market data surfaced first
    • Shadow agents found before an auditor finds them

  • Suitability, reporting and client communications need evidence. Every agent action is logged with the acting user, the data it read and the policy that allowed it.

    • Policy enforced at the moment of action, not after
    • An immutable, exportable audit trail for every action
    • Human approval before anything reaches a client or a trade

  • Research, operations and client teams all run models. Spend is attributed by agent, desk and model, with hard limits that stop an overrun instead of reporting it.

    • Spend by agent, desk, user and model
    • Budget limits per team, enforced by the runtime
    • Value measured against your own evals
