Frequently asked questions

  • Work at Yaju is flexible, collaborative and built on trust. We are headquartered in Barcelona and the team works remotely, so you can work from wherever you are most productive. Every employee receives a workplace stipend, and people in cities without an office can use the coworking benefit.

  • Each team gets an offsite budget so it can gather in person. We understand how much it matters to meet your team face to face, even when you mostly work remotely.

  • We offer a $2,000 annual allowance for learning and education. You can put this towards attending conferences, taking courses, language lessons, or leadership coaching.

  • We aim to keep our interview process clear, respectful and collaborative. It is designed to give you the chance to show your strengths and meet the people you would work with.

    The process starts with an application review, followed by a conversation with a recruiter. From there, you may be asked to complete a take-home assignment, meet with the hiring manager, and then connect with a few team members in your final round of interviews. If everything aligns, we’ll move forward with an offer.

    We’re happy to conduct interviews via video conference and to align sessions to a schedule that suits you (whenever possible).

Beware of scams: Yaju will never ask for payment or third-party services such as CV writing as part of our hiring process. All legitimate roles are listed on this careers page and on LinkedIn only. If you see a role elsewhere, please verify it through the official careers page below.
