We believe that resource access is critical for change to happen.

Yaju Labs Catalyst Grants enable academic partners, civic and public impact organizations to access Yaju's models for free through the Yaju API. The Grant supports initiatives focused on research, public benefit projects, global challenges, among others. Please don't hesitate to share your project even if it doesn't fit these categories directly—we're open to impact in all its forms.

Important - please read before applying:

These grants provide free access to Yaju APIs and are designed to support impact projects and research, with the goal of driving positive social impact or academic advancement. These grants are not intended for commercial organizations or projects. For commercial inquiries, please contact our team at contact@capconsultor.eu. Successful applications will be awarded Grants in the form of API credit applied to a Yaju user account, please ensure you have created an account here to receive the credits.


Please note that Catalyst Grant Credits are subject to rate limits, learn more here.
